⚓ BRIEFING — OPERATION BLACKWATER

The enemy fleet is hiding in fog-filled waters. Locate and destroy their flagship.

  • Click a card to select it, then click the board to target.
  • Press R to rotate line cards between Horizontal / Vertical.
  • Sonar cards reveal contact count — not exact positions.
  • Torpedo fires from your flagship along a row or column (≤6 cells).
  • Decoy Buoy — watch the AI Intel Panel update on the right. The AI does not know it's false.
  • Press ENTER or click END TURN when done.
  • The AI only knows what you let it observe. Check the Reason Log to verify.

⊘ Enable the timer once you're comfortable. It starts disabled.
